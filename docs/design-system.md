# RelocateFlow UI direction

## Product principles

1. Reduce anxiety through clear next actions.
2. Make complex relocation information scannable.
3. Use progressive disclosure instead of showing everything at once.
4. Keep every decision grounded in the customer profile.

## Visual system

- Deep navy for confidence and structure
- Violet for primary actions and personalized guidance
- Mint for completed and healthy states
- Peach for attention and deadlines
- Rounded, layered surfaces with restrained shadows
- Manrope for display typography and DM Sans for interface copy

## Responsive behavior

The landing page, onboarding flow, and dashboard each adapt independently for desktop, tablet, and mobile. Complex dashboard grids collapse into readable single-column cards, while the timeline becomes vertical on small screens.
